Getting Started

1. Create Your Team

Open Valor.Live and sign up as Admin. You'll get a unique 6-character team code to share with your crew.

2. Invite Your Crew

Share your team code with your broadcast team. They sign up with the code and you approve them from the admin panel.

3. Go Live

On broadcast day, tap Go Live. Your entire team sees the status instantly. Use push-to-talk to communicate in real time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does push-to-talk work?

Push-to-talk uses your local WiFi network to send real-time audio between team members. All devices must be connected to the same WiFi network. Hold the microphone button to talk, release to stop.

How many team members can I add?

There is no limit to the number of team members. Your entire broadcast crew can join using your team code.

Does the app work on iPad?

Yes. Valor.Live supports both iPhone and iPad. The iPad version features a sidebar navigation layout optimized for larger screens.

Can I have multiple organizations?

Yes. Each organization has its own team code and data. Members can belong to different organizations.

How do I reset my PIN?

If you're an admin, you can reset your PIN using Google Sign-In from the login screen. Team members should contact their admin to reset their PIN.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure. Data is encrypted in transit and organized by organization so only your team members can access it.

How do I delete my account?

Contact your organization admin to remove your account, or email us at support@valor.live and we'll handle it within 48 hours.

System Requirements

iOS 17.0 or later. Compatible with iPhone and iPad. Push-to-talk requires all devices to be on the same WiFi network.
