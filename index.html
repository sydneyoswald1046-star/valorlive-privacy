Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 28, 2026

Valor.Live ("we", "our", "us") operates the Valor.Live mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

When you create an account on Valor.Live, we collect the following information:

  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Role and team assignment within your organization
  • A 4-digit PIN for authentication

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your identity and provide access to your organization's broadcast team
  • Display your name and role to other team members within your organization
  • Enable team communication features including messaging and push-to-talk
  • Send you notifications related to broadcast events and segment changes
  • Provide equipment tracking and assignment features

Microphone Access

Valor.Live requests access to your device's microphone solely for the push-to-talk (PTT) feature, which allows real-time voice communication with your broadcast team members on the same local network. Audio is transmitted in real-time and is not recorded or stored.

Local Network Access

Valor.Live uses your local network to discover and connect with nearby team members for push-to-talk audio communication. This uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework and operates only on your local WiFi network. No data leaves your local network for this feature.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore) cloud infrastructure. Data is organized by organization and is only accessible to members of your organization. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is shared only with:

  • Other members of your organization (name, role, team, and status)
  • Google Firebase — our cloud infrastructure provider, for data storage and authentication
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to use Google authentication for admin account creation or PIN reset

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active within an organization. If you leave an organization or request account deletion, your data will be removed from that organization's records. Contact your organization administrator or email us to request data deletion.

Children's Privacy

Valor.Live is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. Authentication is handled through PIN-based login and optional Google Sign-In. All data transmission between the app and our servers is encrypted.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy within the app. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.

Contact Us
